# Scene provenance

The reference scene renderer and embedded illustrations are adapted from the publicly available Tiny Story project at https://github.com/bb99kra/20-10, retrieved on 2026-10-20. Its README identifies the project as “Sự thức giấc của Tiny - Ngày 20/10”. The original demonstration URL visible in the user's reference is https://20-10-vuive.kazvndc-edu.workers.dev/.

Original attribution comments are retained in `dist/experience.html`, including the Uiverse starfield attribution to sinful_9361/jaykdoe under the MIT license, and the flower animation attribution to septiandwica/kado / Md Usman Ansari.

Courier Prime is from Google Fonts. Its license is included alongside the font.

The surrounding player, interactive scene clock, reference adapter, fuel-button gate and letter are local additions. V13 restores the native awakening and introduction sequence and aligns the opening scene cues, including the pink transition at 9.7 seconds, to the user's uploaded reference. Text uses the user's requested bà–tui pronouns and chosen gift line. The continuation uses the existing Tiny clover, aircraft and moon/rover artwork plus a locally authored letter; the reference movie itself is not embedded.

The entire soundtrack remains the unaltered audio from the user's uploaded `1000000477.mp4` (97.634 seconds), extracted by AAC stream copy as `nhac-tu-video-1000000477.m4a`. Its decoded audio hash matches the uploaded movie. The recording's own introductory music transition is retained. Audio time drives the story until the interactive fuel stop; the soundtrack then continues independently while the story waits for the recipient to press the fuel button. The former v7/v11 audio files and upstream soundtrack are not shipped or played.

V17 adds scene fade overrides, motion-clock pacing, idle resource preparation, cached fuel geometry and letter opening animation. Existing scene artwork, text choices, story cues and source audio are retained.
